🏰 The Clan Update

What's in the update?

More ways to build a clan and work toward shared goals.

CLAN SYSTEM
🛠️

Create and customize

Submit a clan creation request with a name, description, and your own PNG, JPEG, or WebP icon. Approved creators become clan owners.

🤝

Invitations and join requests

Clan owners can invite server members. Players can also request to join a clan, and its owner can approve or decline the request.

🍬

Shared Candy progress

Eligible Candy earned by members after joining contributes toward the clan's shared milestones. Candy already in a wallet before joining doesn't count.

🏆

Member contribution board

See clan members, their contribution totals, and a ranked board that helps everyone track their part in the clan's progress.

✨

Clan identity and verification

Clan pages show the clan icon, description, membership, and supported verification badges so each clan can stand out.

⚙️

Owner management tools

Clan owners can update the clan name, description, and icon, send invitations, and review requests to join their clan.

Work together. Unlock more.

Reach the configured clan milestones to activate these bonuses.

SHARED REWARDS
10% OFF

Clan shop discount

Reach the clan's shop milestone to unlock a 10% discount on eligible shop purchases.

1.25×

Enhanced daily reward

Reach the daily-reward milestone to unlock a 1.25× daily reward multiplier for eligible clan members.

How to get started

Your next steps are simple.

STEP 01

Open the Clans page

Sign in to the dashboard and choose a server where you and the Aureolis bot are both members.

STEP 02

Create or find a clan

Submit a creation request, or browse existing clans and request to join one.

STEP 03

Earn Candy together

Build shared progress with eligible Candy earnings and work toward your clan's reward milestones.

📌 Good to know: Clan creation requests require approval. Clan reward milestones depend on the configured thresholds, and only eligible Candy earned after joining contributes to clan progress.
