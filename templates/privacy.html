This policy explains what Aureolis collects when you use the bot or the dashboard, why, who can see it, and what choices you have.

✅ What we useYour Discord profile basics, the servers you share with the bot, and your Candy activity.
🚫 What we never ask forYour email, password, payment details, or the ability to read your private messages.
🤝 What we don’t doWe don’t sell your data, run ads, or use analytics or tracking cookies.

1. What we collect

When you use the bot in a Discord server

  • Your Discord user ID, username and display name, and the ID of the server you used the bot in.
  • Your Candy balance, your inventory (items you bought), and when you last claimed your daily reward.
  • An activity history of what happens on your account, such as daily claims, trick-or-treat rewards, Candy transfers, shop purchases, Arcane level-up bonuses, and changes made by administrators. Each entry has your ID, username, the server, an amount where relevant, and the time.
  • Administrator controls that apply to you, such as being blocked from earning Candy or hidden from leaderboards, and who set them.
  • Whether you claimed a role from the role shop.
  • Your authorization record: your Discord user ID and username, which version of these terms you accepted and when, and that you enabled direct messages.

To award Arcane level-up bonuses, the bot reads level-up messages posted by the Arcane bot. It does not store the content of other people’s messages.

When you log in to the dashboard

You sign in with Discord (the identify and guilds permissions). Discord shares with us your basic public profile (such as your ID, username, display name and avatar) and the list of servers you are in, with your permission level in each. We do not receive your email or password, and we cannot read your messages or act on your behalf. The one-time access code Discord gives us is used right away and is not stored.

Authorizing to use the bot

The first time you use a slash command, we ask you to authorize with Discord (the identify permission only), accept the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy, and enable direct messages. We keep a record of that so we don’t ask again. If we change these documents in a meaningful way, we may ask you to accept them again.

Direct messages

With your permission, the bot may send you direct messages, for example to confirm your authorization or to tell you when an administrator adds or removes Candy from your balance. The bot can only send messages to you. It cannot read your private messages. Whether a DM is delivered also depends on your Discord privacy settings.

Cookies

We use one essential session cookie to keep you logged in. It is marked HttpOnly and Secure, and the session data it points to is stored in our database for up to 31 days. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Technical data

Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, pages requested and timestamps, for running and securing the service.

2. How we use it

  • To run Aureolis: balances, daily rewards, the shop, quests, inventory, statistics and leaderboards.
  • To show you your own profile and the servers you can manage.
  • To keep things fair, spot abuse, and let administrators moderate.
  • To fix problems and keep the service secure.

3. Who can see it

  • Other members: leaderboards and statistics can show your name and Candy totals to people using the dashboard or bot in your server.
  • Administrators: the Aureolis team can view user lists and activity history. Administrators of a server can choose to post activity logs into a Discord channel or webhook. Those log messages can include your Discord mention, username, amounts and details, and are visible to anyone who can see that channel.
  • Nobody else: we do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

4. Services we rely on

To run Aureolis we use these providers, which process data on our behalf or when you visit:

  • Discord: login, the bot itself, and avatar images served from Discord’s servers.
  • Railway: hosts the bot and website.
  • MongoDB Atlas: stores balances, activity history and login sessions.
  • UptimeRobot: checks that the site is online. It only requests the site, with no personal data.
  • Google Fonts: our pages load their font from Google, so your browser contacts Google, which can see your IP address.
  • GitHub: stores the project’s source code. User data is not stored there.

These providers have their own privacy policies.

5. How long we keep it

Candy balances, inventories and activity history are kept while Aureolis is running so your progress is preserved, and may be reset or deleted when a season ends. Login sessions expire after 31 days or when you log out.

6. Your choices

  • Log out at any time from the dashboard.
  • Revoke access in Discord under Settings → Authorized Apps.
  • Ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it by contacting us in the Aureolis Discord server. Leaving a server does not delete your data automatically, so please ask.

Deleting your data can remove your Candy, inventory and ranking permanently.

7. Security

We use HTTPS, keep secrets out of public code, and limit who can reach admin tools. No online service is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

8. Children

Aureolis is meant for people who are old enough to use Discord (at least 13 in most countries). If you believe a younger child’s data has been collected, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Aureolis changes. The “last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. See also our Terms of Service.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us in the Aureolis Discord server.
