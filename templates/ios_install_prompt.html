Add Aureolis to your Home Screen Open your Halloween dashboard like an app.

First open this website in Safari to follow these steps.

  1. In Safari, tap Share (the square with an up arrow), or Page Menu → Share.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Turn on Open as Web App if available, then tap Add.
